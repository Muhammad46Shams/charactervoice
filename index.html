Why Cartoon Character Fun?

What is Cartoon Character Fun?

It's a free character generator for everyday things. There are 89 hand-drawn characters across eight groups (animals, fruits, food, vegetables, drinks, desserts, birds and sea creatures), and each one can be given a personality, a line of dialogue and a voice in a couple of taps.

How does it work?

Pick a character and the generator gives it a random personality such as Grumpy, Sassy or Overdramatic. The personality changes the drawing: angry characters frown, worried ones raise their brows and sly ones smirk. The character then says an original line written for its mood and category, followed by a joke, while its mouth moves in time with the words.

Where does the voice come from?

The voice is made by your own browser's built-in speech engine, so nothing you hear is uploaded or recorded. Choose a kid or teen voice and one of 16 languages; the exact sound depends on the voices installed on your device. Lip-sync follows the spoken words, using a separate mouth shape for sounds like "ah", "oh", "ee" and "m".

Who is it for?

Anyone who wants a quick laugh, plus writers looking for a story prompt, teachers warming up a class, and families playing together. Everything is family-friendly, there's no sign-up, and the characters can be shared with one tap.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Cartoon Character Fun free to use?

Yes! Cartoon Character Fun is completely free to use. Generate as many characters as you want, share them with friends, and enjoy the fun.

Do I need to create an account?

No account is required. Simply visit our website and start generating characters immediately. We respect your privacy and don't require personal information to use the generator.

What languages are supported?

We support 15+ languages including English (US, UK, Ireland), Spanish, French, German, Italian, Portuguese, Dutch, Polish, Russian, Japanese, Korean, Chinese (Mandarin), Hindi, and Arabic.

Can I use characters for commercial purposes?

Characters generated on Cartoon Character Fun are for personal and non-commercial use. For commercial use, please contact us for licensing information.

How do I share my characters?

After generating a character, use our social media share buttons to post to Twitter, Facebook, Pinterest, WhatsApp, Reddit, or LinkedIn. You can also copy the character description to share anywhere.

Is the content family-friendly?

Yes. The drawings and character lines are written for all ages, and the bonus jokes come from a joke service with its safe mode switched on.
