About Cartoon Character Fun

What it is

Cartoon Character Fun is a free, family-friendly character generator. You pick one of 89 hand-drawn characters (a banana, a panda, a cup of coffee, an octopus…), the generator gives it a personality, and the character tells you about itself out loud while its mouth moves in time with the words.

It's built for quick laughs, but people also use it as a story prompt, a classroom warm-up or a game with kids: "What would a Grumpy Pineapple say?"

How it's made

  • Hand-drawn characters – every character is drawn as vector art for this site, with its own eyes, brows and mouth so it can blink, react and talk.
  • Moods you can see – the random adjective (Grumpy, Sassy, Overdramatic…) changes the face: angry brows, worried looks or a sly smirk.
  • Original lines – each character says a line written for its mood and its group (animals, desserts, sea creatures and so on), followed by a bonus joke.
  • Voices from your browser – speech comes from your device's built-in voices (the Web Speech API) in 16 languages, styled as a kid or teen voice. Nothing you hear is recorded or uploaded.
  • Lip-sync – the mouth uses different shapes for sounds like "ah", "oh", "ee", "f" and "m", timed to the words being spoken.

What we care about

  • 😂 Fun for everyone – no sign-up, no paywall, and content that's suitable for all ages.
  • 🔒 Privacy – no accounts and no personal details needed to use the generator. See the privacy policy for how ads and cookies work on this site.
  • 🎨 Care over quantity – each character is drawn and animated by hand rather than pulled from a stock library.

How the site is funded

Cartoon Character Fun is an independent project made in Ireland. It's free to use and is supported by advertising, which pays for hosting and new characters.

What's new

  • November 2026 – A brighter, more compact design, a picture-based character picker, and each blog article on its own page.
