🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to The Dramatic Plate (the "Website"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring that your personal information is handled in a safe and responsible manner.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you visit our website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Information you provide voluntarily: When you contact us via email or our contact form, we collect your name, email address, and any message you send.
  • Automatically collected information: Like most websites, we collect standard web log information such as your IP address, browser type, pages visited, and time spent on our site.
  • Cookies: We use minimal cookies to improve your experience. You can disable cookies in your browser settings at any time.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Respond to your inquiries and messages
  • Improve our website and user experience
  • Monitor site usage and performance
  • Send occasional updates (only if you've opted in)

We do NOT: sell, rent, or share your personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services to enhance our website:

  • JokeAPI – For generating funny character quotes. Learn more
  • Dog API – For animal images. Learn more
  • Web Speech API – For voice synthesis. This runs locally in your browser and no data is sent to our servers.

These services may collect anonymous data as per their own privacy policies.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of any inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Opt-out of any marketing communications

To exercise these rights, please contact us.

7. Children's Privacy

Our website is designed for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If we become aware that we have collected personal data from a child, we will delete it promptly.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page with an updated date.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
